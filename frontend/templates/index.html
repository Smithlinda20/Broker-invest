FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start investing.

How do I get started with Broker Invest?

+
Simply create a free account, choose your investment plan, make a deposit using your preferred payment method, and your returns start accruing the same day. The entire process takes under 5 minutes.

When and how can I withdraw my earnings?

+
You can request a withdrawal at any time, 24/7. Withdrawals are processed within 10–30 minutes to your chosen cryptocurrency wallet or bank account. There is no minimum withdrawal waiting period once your plan generates returns.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT (Tether), and direct bank transfers. All transactions are processed securely and your funds are credited to your account instantly upon blockchain confirmation.

How does the referral program work?

+
When you refer a friend using your unique referral link and they make an investment, you earn 10% of their investment amount as a commission — credited instantly to your account. There is no cap on how much you can earn through referrals.

Is my investment capital returned after the plan ends?

+
Yes. At the end of your investment plan's duration, your original principal is returned to your account balance along with any accrued returns. You can then reinvest or withdraw your full balance.

Can I run multiple investment plans simultaneously?

+
Absolutely. You can invest in multiple plans at the same time to diversify your portfolio and maximize your daily returns. Each plan operates independently and earns its respective daily percentage.
